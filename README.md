# Improved Lessons

Coursebook lessons made interactive for EOI students. Click any link to open the lesson directly in your browser — no login or download needed.

|---|---|---|---|

| The Surgery — Present Perfect | B2.1 | Team game set in a doctor’s surgery for practising the present perfect simple and continuous (English File 2A). | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/B21-2A-the-surgery-present-perfect.html) |
| have — Lexical and Grammatical Uses | B2.2 | Lesson on the uses of have as a main verb and as an auxiliary. Every right answer helps fill the coffee cup. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/B22-have-lesson.html) |
| The Prism — Pronouns, it and there | B2.2 | Discovery-based lesson on generic, reflexive and reciprocal pronouns and on it and there, organised in stations with a summary of the rules. Saves progress. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/B22-pronouns-the-prism.html) |
| The Connector Café — Discourse Markers | B2.2 | Four-step lesson on joiners and signposts, with a word list, practice and a results page. Works in class or for self-study. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/connector-cafe.html) |
| Past Lines — Narrative Tenses on the Move | B2.1 | Travel-themed lesson on the four narrative tenses (Unit 3A), laid out as a transport network. Students read a story, practise on short travel tales, work on so and such, and finish by telling their own travel story. Play alone or in teams; mistakes come back for review. Saves progress. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/b21-narrative-tenses-travel.html) |
| get — One Verb, Five Jobs | B2.2 | Lesson on the five uses of get, be or get, and get something done / get someone to do. Includes a review story, speaking cards, notes on UK and US use and for Spanish and Catalan speakers, and a round to practise your mistakes. Saves progress. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/b22-uses-of-get.html) |
| A Twist in the Tale — Adverbs and False Friends | B2.1 | Lesson on adverbs and adverbial phrases (English File 3B) told through very short stories with a twist: types of adverb, where they go, pairs that are easy to confuse, and English that sounds translated from Catalan or Spanish. Ends with students finishing a scene and cutting a story to 50 words. Saves progress. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/b21-adverbs-twist-in-the-tale.html) |
| The Connector Café · Part 2 — Discourse Markers | B2.2 | Follow-on to The Connector Café (File 3B): markers to change the subject, go back to a point, add something, make yourself clearer and sum up, taught through conversations overheard at the café tables. Saves progress. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/b22-connector-cafe-part-2.html) |
| The Connector Café · The Regulars | B2.2 | Game that goes with both Connector Café lessons: the regulars’ conversations keep going off course, and players choose the marker that puts each one back on track. Several modes, with sound and full-screen for the whiteboard. | [Open](https://as1-design.github.io/improvedlessons/b22-connector-cafe-regulars.html) |
