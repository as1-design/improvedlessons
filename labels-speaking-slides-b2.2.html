Labels I've got

  • I've got a very … side.
  • I've got a heart of gold / a quick temper.
  • On the whole, I'm quite …
👀

Labels others give me

  • People say I've got …, but deep down …
  • I tend to come across as …
  • On the surface, I seem …
✂️

Labels I'd take off

  • I haven't really got …
  • I don't have … any more.
  • I used to be a bit too …
Then Choose three sentences and secretly change one so it's false.

Both are correct

British EnglishAmerican English
I've got a quick temper.I have a quick temper.
Have you got a sarcastic side?Do you have a sarcastic side?
I haven't got much patience.I don't have much patience.

I have / Do you have…? is also very common in the UK. In the US, have got sounds informal. Pick one and be consistent in the same sentence.

3Reveal the false one and tell the story behind the true ones.
4Swap.
